---------------------------------- wildcard operators in Scala SQL (Spark SQL) ----------------------------------

1. % (Percentage wildcard)
            SELECT * FROM Table1
            WHERE city LIKE 'D%';

_ (Underscore wildcard)
            SELECT * FROM Table1
            WHERE city LIKE 'M_m_ai';

    ----*---*----*---*----*---*----*---*----*----*---*----*---*----*----
            Query pattern	Meaning
                    'A%'	Starts with A
                    '%a'	Ends with a
                    '%an%'	Contains an
                    'A___'	Starts with A and has exactly 4 characters
                    '____'	Exactly 4 characters
                    '_a%'	Second character is a

    ----*---*----*---*----*---*----*---*----*----*---*----*---*----*----


scala> spark.sql("select* from Table1 where city like 'c%'").show()
